Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm not tech-savvy?

That's exactly who we built this service for. You don't need to understand AI, coding, or automation platforms. We handle the entire technical process — from analysis to deployment. You just tell us what tasks consume your time, and we build the system to handle them.

Which AI tools do you use?

We use enterprise-grade AI tools including Claude AI, ChatGPT API, and Perplexity for intelligence tasks. For automation orchestration, we work with Make.com, Zapier, and n8n. The specific combination depends on your workflow requirements — we always recommend the best tool for each job.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. We implement multiple layers of security: 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transfers, secure API connections with OAuth where possible, and strict access controls. Your data is never used to train AI models, and we offer NDA agreements for sensitive engagements.

What if the automation breaks?

We build robust systems with comprehensive error handling, but if something does break, you're covered. During your 90-day support period, we diagnose and fix any issues at no additional cost, typically within 24 hours. We also build in monitoring so many issues are caught before you even notice them.

Can I modify automations later?

Yes, and we teach you how. During handoff, we provide comprehensive documentation showing exactly how to make common adjustments. For simple changes, you'll be fully self-sufficient. For complex modifications, our support team is available during your 90-day support window.

Do you provide team training?

Yes, training is included in all plans. Our Professional and Enterprise plans include multiple dedicated training sessions. We record all sessions for future reference and provide written documentation your team can refer to at any time.

How do you handle credentials and passwords?

We never ask for your passwords. We use secure OAuth connections wherever possible — Gmail, Notion, Slack, and most major platforms support this. For services that require API keys, we guide you through generating them yourself and entering them directly into the automation platform.

What if I'm not satisfied with the results?

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee. If your AI system doesn't save you at least 5 hours in your first month, we'll refund every rupee — no questions asked. We're confident in our delivery because we don't proceed with the build until you've approved the blueprint.

Can you integrate with my existing tools?

Almost certainly. Through Make.com and Zapier, we can connect with over 5,000 applications. Popular integrations include Gmail, Outlook, Google Sheets, Notion, Slack, Trello, Asana, HubSpot, and many more. During the discovery call, we'll confirm compatibility with your specific tool stack.
